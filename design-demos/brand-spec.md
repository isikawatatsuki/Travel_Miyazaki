# Brand spec for the direction study

## Identity

- Name: 旅のしおり
- English utility label: Travel Tickets
- Existing mark: `../public/icons/favicon.svg`, a green booklet with a pink rule and yellow bookmark.
- Existing visual memory: warm paper, travel ticket, itinerary lines, green ink, small pink/yellow/blue accents.

## Existing palette sampled from the product

- Paper: `#fffef9`
- Canvas: `#f6f5ef`
- Ink: `#18342b`
- Green: `#23745b`
- Pink: `#d97687`
- Yellow: `#f1c75b`
- Blue: `#4a97b5`

Each direction may remap tone and proportion, but must derive its colors from this set rather than inventing an unrelated purple SaaS palette.

## Type and assets

- Japanese copy must remain the primary language.
- Use system-safe Japanese stacks in the standalone drafts; do not depend on a network font.
- Use the existing SVG mark when a product mark is needed.
- No destination photography is required for this direction study: the subject is a travel-management tool, and the actual information hierarchy—not decorative stock imagery—is the content.

## Do not

- Do not change features, sample trip facts, or navigation labels.
- Do not use emoji as interface icons.
- Do not add invented metrics or testimonials.
- Do not use generic purple gradients, excessive glass cards, or ornamental travel photography.
